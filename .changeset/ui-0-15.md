learn-sdk now depends on `@hussmartinez/ui` ^0.15.0 and `@hussmartinez/sdk-core` ^0.4.7. If your app also depends on ui or sdk-core, directly or through another `@hussmartinez` SDK, move it to those ranges too, so the app keeps a single copy of each.

Screen readers now hear the player's announcements as sentences. ui 0.15 says messages sent in the same frame together, so the last exercise's result and the summary are no longer cut short. To make the joined text read well, the player announces a check with two new messages, `player.announceCorrect` ("Correct.") and `player.announceResult` ("{score} of {max} right."), and `wordOrder.placed`, `wordOrder.returned` and `wordOrder.moved` end with a full stop. If you override any of these in `messages`, end your text with your language's punctuation too. The visible `player.correct` and `player.result` text is unchanged.

The player's Previous, Next and Skip icons now point the right way in right-to-left pages, because ui mirrors them there. Nothing else changes in how the player looks.
