`LearnPlayer` lays out each checked item more clearly: the verdict ("Correct" or "Not quite", with its icon) on its own line, then the right answer and any explanation, each on a line of its own, where they used to run on in one sentence. An explanation is no longer marked as text in the learned language. Typed gaps are about a word wide and grow with what's typed, rather than a fixed wide field, and content that can't be opened is shown in a panel of its own. The summary panel uses the raised surface, with a border.
